# LightPlayer 1.1.0

[简体中文](v1.1.0.md) | English

Adds a weather theme, desktop lyrics, private browsing and more, improves the playlist and lyrics display, and fixes a number of problems.

## Download and install

- For Macs with **Apple Silicon (M-series chips)**, running **macOS 12** or later.
- Download `LightPlayer_1.1.0_aarch64.dmg` below, open it and drag LightPlayer into Applications (it can replace the old version; your library and settings are kept).
- This version is not notarized by Apple. If macOS says it is "damaged" or the developer "can't be verified" the first time you open it, right-click LightPlayer in Applications and choose "Open", or run in Terminal:

  ```
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/LightPlayer.app
  ```

## New features

- **Weather theme**: choose "Weather" under "Settings > Appearance > Mode".
  - The app gets the live weather where you are and turns the whole interface into a matching animated sky: sunshine, gauzy bands of cloud that change with the weather, rain, thunder and lightning, snow, hail, fog, and the moon and stars at night; dawn and dusk colors change too.
  - Raindrops, snowflakes and hail land on the playback bar, the cover, dialogs and other parts of the interface: rain splashes, snow builds up a thin layer and slowly melts, hail bounces.
  - The title bar shows the current weather and temperature; click it for the feels-like temperature, humidity, wind speed and sunrise/sunset times.
  - The first time, macOS asks whether LightPlayer may use your location; if not, an approximate location from the network is used, or you can choose a city in Settings.
  - Weather data comes from Open-Meteo and place names from BigDataCloud, both free and without an account.
- **Desktop lyrics**: the desktop lyrics button in the playback bar opens a lyrics strip floating above all windows.
  - The desktop lyrics keep scrolling while the app is minimized or playing in the background with its window closed.
  - One line at a time, animated between lines.
  - Hover to change the color or close it at the top right; drag to move it, and drag the bottom-right corner to resize (the font size follows); the position and size are remembered.
  - The lyrics page now opens from the song info at the bottom left of the playback bar (or press Y).
- **Playlist**:
  - Drag the handle on the left to reorder; hover an item and click "×", or right-click it, to remove it from the list (the file is not deleted).
  - Right-click also offers "Play next".
  - The playlist can show on the lyrics page too; when the window is narrow it appears as a floating panel instead of squeezing the lyrics.
- **Lyric highlight color**: the color dot at the top right of the lyrics page (or "Settings > Lyrics") changes the color of the current lyric line.
- **Library**:
  - Albums can be removed from the library (the files stay, and refreshing the folders won't add them back) or moved straight to the Trash; right-click an album, or click "More" on the album page.
  - Next to "Playlists" in the sidebar you can import a folder as a playlist: its music and videos are added to the library and a playlist named after the folder is created.
- **Make LightPlayer the default player in one click**: click "Set up" under "Settings > Playback > File associations" and common audio and video formats open in LightPlayer by default; double-click a file to play it.
- **Continuous play and videos in the background**: under "Settings > Playback" you can choose separately whether music and videos play the next item when one ends (by default music continues and videos stop), and whether videos keep playing in the background after the window is closed (by default videos pause and music keeps playing).
- **NetEase Cloud Music (experimental)**: once turned on under "Settings > NetEase Cloud Music", sign in on NetEase Cloud Music's official web page (password, SMS code or QR code) or by scanning a QR code with your phone, then play your playlists, liked songs and daily picks from the library sidebar, or search for songs; lyrics come from NetEase Cloud Music.
  - This feature uses unofficial NetEase Cloud Music web APIs that may stop working at any time; using a third-party client also breaks NetEase Cloud Music's terms of service, and your account could be restricted.
  - Member-only songs play in full only with a member account, otherwise only a preview clip plays; when a song can't be played or only a clip can, the reason NetEase Cloud Music gives is shown. LightPlayer does not download or crack any songs.
- **Private browsing**: when on, opened files don't appear in recently played, play counts and positions aren't recorded, and nothing is added to the library automatically. Switch it under "Settings > Playback > Privacy", from the menu bar icon's menu, or with ⇧⌘N.

## Fixes

- **Lyrics getting faster and faster**: some files downloaded from music platforms made the playback clock drift, so lyrics matched at the start but ran ahead later on. The app now detects such files and switches to precise timing; if they still don't line up, turn on "Precise timing" in the "…" menu at the top right of the lyrics page.
- **Lyrics out of sync after seeking**: after clicking a lyric, dragging the progress bar or skipping 15 seconds, MP3 and FLAC sometimes resumed slightly early or late, so the lyrics ran ahead of or behind the singing. The app now prepares a precisely seekable copy in the background once playback starts, and later seeks resume at the exact position.
- **Double-clicking a file only opened the main window**: double-clicking a media file while the app wasn't running started the app without playing the file. It now starts playing.
- **Lyric preview on the player page**: the cover and song info shifted slightly when the lyric changed; now they stay put, and lyrics fade in and out.
- **Couldn't get back to a video**: after going back to the library while a video played, clicking the video info at the left of the playback bar did nothing. Clicking it now returns to the video, and the top of the library sidebar shows "Playing a video".

## Other changes

- The top of the lyrics page shows only the title and artist, no cover.
- Blank lines in lyrics (instrumental breaks) no longer show a music note placeholder; the previous line stays until the next one starts.
- The avatars on the library's "Artists" page are half the size.
- In full-screen video, the exit button at the top right is gone; use the button in the playback bar or press Esc.

## Known limitations

- Not notarized by Apple; the first time you open it, allow it as described above.
- Only an Apple Silicon version for now, no Intel version.
- When a file is opened by double-clicking it in Finder, macOS itself may add it to "Recent Items"; this is outside private browsing's control.
- AI recognition needs a model download the first time (the recommended model is about 550 MB); a mirror for mainland China can be chosen in Settings.
- The bundled ffmpeg is a GPL build, for personal use only.
- Picture subtitles such as PGS and VobSub are not supported yet.
