# LightPlayer 1.6.9

[简体中文](v1.6.9.md) | English

Adds the Archive theme: the current playlist becomes a 3D array of archive boxes, and clicking a box plays that song. The macOS and Windows versions are updated together.

## Download and install

### macOS

- For Macs with **Apple Silicon (M-series chips)**, running **macOS 12** or later.
- Download `LightPlayer_1.6.9_aarch64.dmg` below, open it and drag LightPlayer into Applications (it can replace the old version; your library and settings are kept).
- This version is not notarized by Apple. If macOS says it is "damaged" or the developer "can't be verified" the first time you open it, right-click LightPlayer in Applications and choose "Open", or run in Terminal:

  ```
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/LightPlayer.app
  ```

### Windows

- For **Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit)**.
- **Installer (recommended)**: download `LightPlayer_1.6.9_x64-setup.exe` and run it. It installs for the current user without administrator rights, adds LightPlayer to the Start menu, and can be uninstalled under "Settings > Apps".
- **Portable**: download `LightPlayer_1.6.9_x64_portable.zip`, unzip it and run `LightPlayer.exe` in the folder (keep `ffmpeg.exe`, `ffprobe.exe` and `aria2c.exe` next to it).
- The installer is not code-signed. If you see "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".
- The Microsoft Edge WebView2 runtime is needed: Windows 11 and recent Windows 10 include it; otherwise the installer downloads and installs it, and for the portable version please install it from Microsoft's website first.

## New

- **Archive theme**: choose "Archive" under "Settings > Appearance > Mode".
  - The player page fills with white archive boxes in slanted rows. Each box is a song in the current playlist; a short playlist repeats to fill the window.
  - Click a box to play that song. The wheel moves up and down a column; hold Shift while scrolling, or scroll sideways, to move to another column. You can also drag the array.
  - The box under the pointer lifts, and choosing a box sends a ripple out around it.
  - The chosen song's title, artist and length show right of center, and the playing song's title, artist and progress show at the bottom left. Nothing else is written on the page.
  - The title bar and play bar stay hidden until the pointer nears the top or bottom of the window. The play bar also shows for a moment when you press a shortcut.
  - The library, the lyrics page and videos show as usual; the array behind them pauses and blurs.
  - With this theme chosen, two settings appear below it: "Floating boxes" (the array sways gently while playing) and "Quality" (Power saving / Standard / Fine, Standard by default).
  - The theme makes no sound.
  - Inspired by [LBEILC/RhineLabUI](https://github.com/LBEILC/RhineLabUI); the archive box model comes from that project (MIT license).
- **Plugins**: new anchors `archive`, `archive-now` and `archive-selected`; `data-bg` can be `archive`; `theme` can be set to `archive`. See the [plugin docs](https://github.com/2001020/LightPlayer/blob/lightplayer-main/docs/plugins/README.en.md).

## Upgrading

- Users of 1.6.0 or later (betas included) can update in the app (Settings > About > Check for updates). 1.6.2 and later download this update over several connections with aria2.
- Upgrading from 1.5.0 or earlier needs a manual download.

## Known limitations

- The 3D array needs WebGL. On a computer without it, the player page shows a single line of text instead; everything else works.
- "Fine" quality uses a lot of power on integrated graphics; on battery, "Standard" or "Power saving" is better.
- For LAN sync playback, see the known limitations in [1.6.7](https://github.com/2001020/LightPlayer/releases/tag/v1.6.7).
- Other known limitations are the same as in [1.6.0](https://github.com/2001020/LightPlayer/releases/tag/v1.6.0).
